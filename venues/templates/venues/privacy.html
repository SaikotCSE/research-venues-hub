Privacy

Your data, plainly explained

VenuePulse collects only what it needs to work. There are no advertising or analytics trackers, and every script, font and icon is served from this site — no third-party CDNs see your visits.

What we store

  • Account: username, email address, a salted password hash, and profile details you choose to add (display name, affiliation, research interests, timezone, reminder preferences).
  • Your work: starred venues and private notes, venue lists, saved searches, papers, submissions and checklists, experience reviews and correction suggestions you submit.
  • Without an account: stars and notes are stored against a random identifier kept in your session cookie, and merged into your account if you sign up.
  • Operational logs: request logs (IP address, path, timing, a request ID) kept briefly to keep the service secure and reliable.

Email

We email you only for account security (confirmation and password resets), deadline reminders and the weekly digest — and reminders only after you confirm your address. Turn reminders and the digest off at any time on your profile.

What others can see

Everything is private by default. Venue lists you make public, and the display name on experience reviews you post, are visible to others. Your calendar-feed and API tokens are secrets — regenerate them on your profile if they leak.

You are in control

From account settings you can download a complete JSON export of your data, change your email or password, and permanently delete your account together with everything associated with it.
